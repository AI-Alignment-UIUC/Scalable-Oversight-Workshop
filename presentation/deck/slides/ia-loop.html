01 · Iterated amplification

Distill the team into X, then amplify again

Step 1

Amplify

Run H plus copies of X on sampled questions. Record the answers.

Step 2

Distill

Train X by supervised learning to give the team’s answers in one fast pass.

Step 3

Repeat

The team gets stronger because X did. X chases a moving target.

No outside objective: “the objective is implicit in the way that the human coordinates the copies of X” (§1.1)

The bet, factored cognition: hard reasoning breaks into small pieces a human can check.

Training loop from §2.2, simplified: the paper also learns a model H′ of the human so H isn’t queried every time. Amplify(X) “is modestly smarter than X working on its own, and X chases this moving target” (§2.3). In the limit the system approximates a huge tree of copies of H. Christiano’s name for that ideal, HCH (“humans consulting HCH”), comes from his blog posts, not this paper.
