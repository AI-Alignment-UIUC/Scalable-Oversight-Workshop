01 · Method

Distill the team into X, then amplify again

Step 1 · §2.2

Amplify

Sample a question, let H answer it with copies of X, and “record every decision made by H during the process.”

Step 2 · §2.2

Model the human

“We train a model H′ to predict the decisions made by H in each of these transcripts”

Step 3 · §2.2

Distill

Amplify again with H′ in place of H. “X is trained by supervised learning on these (Q, A) pairs.”

No outside objective: “the objective is implicit in the way that the human coordinates the copies of X” (§1.1)

Repeat: the amplified team “is modestly smarter than X working on its own, and X chases this moving target” (§2.3)

Why H′ exists (§2.1): “In order to reduce the burden on the human expert H, we train a ‘human predictor’ H′”. Because H′ only learns how to split questions and combine answers, the paper expects “to train it with much less data”. In the limit the system approximates a huge tree of copies of H. Christiano’s name for that ideal, HCH (“humans consulting HCH”), comes from his blog posts, not this paper. Our notebook skips H′: H answers directly.
