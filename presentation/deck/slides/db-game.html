02 · Debate · Method

Two agents argue. A judge decides.

“A question q ∈ Q is shown to both agents. The two agents state their answers a₀, a₁ ∈ A (which may be the same). The two agents take turns making statements s₀, s₁, . . . , sₙ₋₁ ∈ S. The judge sees the debate (q, a, s) and decides which agent wins.”

Irving, Christiano & Amodei (2018), AI Safety via Debate, §2

The judge stands in for a human who could never solve the question alone. The debaters stand in for strong models.

Hubinger’s one-line version (proposal 9): train a model, Alice, to win debates against a copy of itself, Bob, in front of a human judge. The question for today: when does winning the debate line up with telling the truth?
