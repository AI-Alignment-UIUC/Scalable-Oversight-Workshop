03 · What the paper concludes

A research agenda: five challenges, ten approaches

Challenges (§4)

  • Amount of feedback
  • Feedback distribution
  • Reward hacking
  • Unacceptable outcomes
  • Reward-result gap

Approaches (§5)

  • Online feedback
  • Off-policy feedback
  • Leveraging existing data
  • Hierarchical feedback
  • Natural language
  • Model-based RL
  • Side-constraints
  • Adversarial training
  • Uncertainty estimates
  • Inductive bias

No experiments of its own: the direction is “‘shovel-ready’ for empirical research today” (§8). Bold: the pair our demo shows.

Section and subsection titles are verbatim. The §5 approaches “should be thought of as directions to explore”. Section 6, Establishing trust, adds five avenues: design choices, testing, interpretability, formal verification and theoretical guarantees. The outlook (§8): “agent alignment research is still in its early stages, but we believe that there is substantial reason for optimism”.
