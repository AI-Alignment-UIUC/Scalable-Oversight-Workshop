The problem

Human feedback works while humans can check the work

“…there are harder tasks for which we can’t compute demonstrations or rewards even with human assistance.”

Christiano, Shlegeris & Amodei (2018), Supervising strong learners by amplifying weak experts, §1

Too big

Reviewing a 10,000-line pull request

Too hard

Checking a long proof or a novel experiment

Too slow

Judging a policy whose effects take years

RLHF and its relatives depend on a person being able to tell good output from bad. As systems take on bigger and harder tasks, that stops being true. Ask the room for an example from their own work where they could not check an AI’s answer.
